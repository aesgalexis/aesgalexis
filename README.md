# Alexis Santana

Industrial technician, product builder and AI-assisted developer.

I am an industrial technician and the founder of [UNATOMO](https://unatomo.com). After years working with industrial machinery, maintenance, troubleshooting and operational processes, particularly in industrial laundry, I now combine that experience with software and AI to build practical tools for real companies and technical teams.

> I spent years fixing industrial machines. Now I also build the software around them.

## What I work on

- Industrial software and operational tools.
- Machine identification, documentation and maintenance workflows.
- AI-assisted product development.
- Automation and connected-machine experiments.
- Tools that solve concrete business problems.
- Better communication between people, machines and technical information.

## UNATOMO

[UNATOMO](https://unatomo.com) is my main active project and product ecosystem: a practical platform developed from real industrial experience to improve how companies identify, document, understand and manage machines, incidents and technical processes.

## Current direction

I am currently exploring AI-assisted development workflows, web applications and practical internal tools, along with new ways to connect physical industry with lightweight software. This includes using ESP32 boards and external sensors to monitor older industrial machines without interfering with their original automation.

## Technology

Currently building with TypeScript, JavaScript, React, Firebase, ESP32 and AI-assisted development workflows.
